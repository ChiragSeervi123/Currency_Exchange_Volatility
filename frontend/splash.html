How it works

From a screenshot to a full report

  1. 01

    Upload a chart

    Any screenshot: USD/INR, Nifty 50, Bitcoin… Browse, drag and drop, or paste it.

  2. 02

    R reads the pixels

    Candles or the line are found by colour, and the price axis is read with OCR (Tesseract).

  3. 03

    R applies the syllabus

    Moments, Bayes, binomial, Poisson and normal models, Z, t, F, χ² and ANOVA, regression.

  4. 04

    Read the verdict

    Our hypothesis (up, down or close to the mean line) and a report you can download or print.
